# SellBodr — Brand Reference

## Application Name
**SellBodr**
Short name: SellBodr

## Tagline
*Find Products in India. Sell Globally.*

## Description
AI-powered cross-border eCommerce intelligence platform. Discover high-margin India-sourced products to sell on Amazon, Etsy & 70+ global marketplaces.

## Logo Design

- **Shape**: Rounded square background (rx=22)
- **Mark**: Two bezier arcs forming a stylised "S" — bidirectional cross-border trade flow arrows
- **Stroke**: White, stroke-width 9, rounded caps
- **Gradient**: saffron-orange `#f97316` (top-left / India) → deep violet `#7c3aed` → indigo `#4338ca` (bottom-right / Global)

## Usage Rules

- Always display the logo on a dark background (`#020817` or darker).
- App name: **SellBodr** — capital S and B, no spaces, no hyphen.
- Tagline always italicised in marketing copy.
- Never stretch or recolor the logo mark.
