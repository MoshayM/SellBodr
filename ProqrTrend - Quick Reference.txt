──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  OPPORTUNITY SCORING (v2.0.0)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Overall Score  =  Weighted composite of 7 sub-scores (0–100)

  Sub-score       Weight   What it measures
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Demand          25%      Search volume + sales velocity
  Competition     20%      Number & strength of competitors
  Margin          20%      Net profit after all costs
  Trend           15%      Rising / stable / falling interest
  Saturation      10%      Market crowding index
  Shipping        5%       Weight / size / duty feasibility
  Marketplace Fit 5%       Category suitability for the market

  Decision thresholds:
  · LAUNCH  →  Score ≥ 65
  · HOLD    →  Score 40–64
  · REJECT  →  Score < 40
