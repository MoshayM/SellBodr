# vision.md — SellBodr

## 1. Mission

Enable any individual or business in India to discover and launch **profitable cross-border eCommerce products** with the confidence of a seasoned marketplace analyst — automated end to end by AI.

## 2. Vision Statement

> Become the default intelligence layer that powers India → World eCommerce, turning India's manufacturing and artisan supply base into a globally optimized seller economy.

## 3. The Opportunity

India has deep, fragmented, low-cost supply (handicrafts, textiles, home goods, jewelry, leather, wellness, decor). International marketplaces have high willingness-to-pay and high information asymmetry. The arbitrage exists; the **decision intelligence to act on it does not.** SellBodr is that intelligence.

We are deliberately **not** a B2B export marketplace, a freight forwarder, or a generic product-research tool for one marketplace. We are a **cross-border, multi-marketplace, India-sourced opportunity engine.**

## 4. Personas

| Persona | Description | Primary Need |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Solo Arbitrage Seller** | First-time or part-time seller exploring Amazon USA | "Tell me one winning product and exactly how to launch it." |
| **Pro Marketplace Seller** | Runs 10–100 SKUs across marketplaces | Pipeline of validated opportunities + margin precision |
| **Sourcing Agency** | Manages portfolios for multiple seller clients | Multi-user workspaces, portfolio analytics, white-label-lite |
| **Brand / Enterprise** | D2C brand or large seller, data-driven | API access, white-label, bulk scoring, custom marketplaces |

## 5. Value Proposition

- **Discover**: Continuously surface high-opportunity products, not just trending lists.
- **Validate**: A defensible Opportunity Score combining demand, competition, margin, trend, saturation, shipping, and marketplace fit.
- **Source**: Matched Indian suppliers with cost, MOQ, lead time, and export readiness.
- **Profit**: Full landed-cost + fee model producing net profit, ROI, and break-even.
- **Launch**: Ready-to-paste listing, keywords, pricing, and positioning.

## 6. Differentiators

1. **India-sourcing native** — sourcing feasibility is a first-class scoring input, not an afterthought.
2. **Cross-marketplace** — the same product is scored against multiple marketplaces/countries simultaneously.
3. **Agentic** — ten specialized agents instead of one monolithic prompt.
4. **End-to-end** — from discovery to a launch-ready listing in one workflow.
5. **Reproducible scoring** — versioned formulas, auditable decisions.

## 7. Success Metrics (North Stars)

| Metric | Definition | Target (Year 1) |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| **Validated Opportunities Delivered** | Opportunities with score ≥ 80 shown to users | 250k |
| **Launch Conversion** | % of recommended products users mark "launched" | ≥ 15% |
| **Profit Prediction Accuracy** | Predicted vs. realized net margin (MAPE) | ≤ 20% |
| **Activation** | New users reaching first full opportunity report | ≥ 60% |
| **Net Revenue Retention** | | ≥ 110% |

## 8. Non-Goals

- We do **not** handle logistics, customs brokerage, or payments for the physical goods.
- We do **not** guarantee marketplace approval or compliance outcomes (we advise).
- We do **not** sell leads or supplier contacts as a marketplace transaction.

## 9. Guiding Principles

1. **Decisions over data.** Every screen ends in an action.
2. **Show the math.** Scores and profits are always explainable.
3. **Trust through verification.** AI claims are grounded in connector data.
4. **India-first sourcing, world-first selling.**
